Spotify Deck

Spotify's login page doesn't work on iOS 12, so the login comes from a computer:

1. On the computer, open this deck's address and connect Spotify.
2. Hold its top-left corner for 2 seconds and choose Send login to iPad.
3. On this iPad, end Guided Access and delete the Deck icon.
4. Scan the code with the Camera app and tap the banner.
5. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Deck.
